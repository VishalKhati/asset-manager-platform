# Legal checklist (Gate 3)

**This is not legal advice.** It lists the questions to take to a qualified Indian lawyer **before** charging for signals, adding broker or affiliate links, or promoting the service to Indian residents. Until then the service stays free, educational, and without referrals.

## Questions for the lawyer

1. **SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 and SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.** Does publishing buy/sell calls on XAUUSD CFDs, free or paid, make the operator a research analyst or investment adviser? Is registration needed, and what does that require (qualification, certification, net worth, disclosures)?
2. **SEBI's 2024 rules on unregistered "finfluencers".** These restrict regulated entities from associating with unregistered persons who give advice or make performance claims. How do they affect this track record page and any broker partnership?
3. **FEMA and RBI.**
   - Indian residents may trade forex only in permitted pairs on recognised Indian exchanges, and LRS remittances for margin forex or CFD trading are not allowed.
   - Does promoting an offshore CFD broker, or signals meant for it, create liability?
   - Is the chosen broker on the **RBI Alert List** of unauthorised forex platforms?
4. **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.** What is required once user accounts or emails are collected (Phase 11)?
5. **Telegram Terms of Service and channel rules** for financial content.
6. **The broker's terms** on redistributing its prices. The public chart shows only a short window of M5 bars around each signal.

## Until Gate 3 is passed

- No fees, subscriptions, tips or donations tied to signals.
- No broker, affiliate or "open an account" links, and no referral codes.
- Don't target Indian residents for CFD trading.
- Keep the disclaimer on every page and every Telegram post, as the code already does.
- Show every signal, including losses and expired ones, and label backtests as hypothetical.
- Record the lawyer's written answer and the date in PLAN.md.
